As AI gets cheaper, judgment becomes more valuable

July 22, 2026

As AI models become cheaper and more interchangeable, competitive advantage shifts toward better decisions, proprietary data, workflows, and customer relationships.

I read an interesting article arguing that Apple, not the frontier model companies, may end up being the biggest winner in AI.

Maybe. But I think the bigger takeaway is: As AI models become cheaper and more interchangeable, competitive advantage shifts somewhere else.

We’ve seen this before.

  1. Infrastructure becomes less differentiated.
  2. Implementation gets cheaper.
  3. Value moves up the stack.

It means the scarce resource changes.

The question is shifting from ‘Can we build it?’ to ‘Where does it actually create leverage?’

  • What decisions should remain uniquely human?
  • What is our real moat? Proprietary data, workflows, customer relationships?
  • How do we get AI into the hands of customers and employees?

I think that’s the shift many companies are still underestimating.

As software becomes cheaper to produce, the quality of decisions becomes more valuable than the quality of implementation.
