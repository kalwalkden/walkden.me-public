Selected Work

A few examples of decisions I've led and results delivered with the teams involved.

HelloFresh / Factor— stabilizing a platform the business depended on

I joined Factor as CTO to address platform outages that were constraining growth. The outages were happening daily near peak ordering deadlines. I chose an incremental modernization rather than a full rewrite and aligned the team around that direction.

The team replaced critical parts of the platform while keeping the business running. We eliminated peak-period outages and gave the company a more reliable foundation for growth.

I helped build the technology and architecture that supported Factor's growth from $20 million to almost $300 million in annual revenue before the acquisition. My work also included aligning systems, teams, and processes as Factor became part of HelloFresh.

ForeverCar— removing friction from the buying process

A third-party pricing service took over five minutes to return a quote. As CTO, I led its replacement with an internal pricing engine, working with the team to improve the buying experience and expand pricing capabilities.

Quote time fell to under five seconds, and visitor-to-purchase conversion improved by approximately tenfold. The new engine also supported per-customer pricing across multiple providers.

Operational and Fractional Work— improving platforms, teams, and execution

I’ve worked with dozens of startups and growth-stage companies to build products, modernize platforms, and improve execution. That has included launching a first customer-facing platform, validating it with early customers, building an engineering team, and planning legacy modernization without disrupting the business. In manufacturing, I’m replacing ad hoc processes with software that improves estimating, project visibility, and profitability.

The work also extends to how teams operate. At Double Good, I reorganized teams around business domains and replaced 12-month planning cycles with 6–12-week outcome increments. Clearer ownership and shorter planning cycles helped improve development velocity by approximately 30%.
